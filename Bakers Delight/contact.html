Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take custom orders?

Yes! We specialize in custom cakes and large orders. Please use our enquiry form for custom orders to get pricing and availability.

What are your delivery options?

We offer delivery within Johannesburg for orders over R500. Delivery fees start at R50. Same-day delivery available for orders placed before 12pm.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes! We offer a selection of gluten-free baked goods. Please check our products page or ask in-store for daily availability.

Can I pre-order for events?

Absolutely! We recommend placing event orders at least 48 hours in advance. For large events, 1-2 weeks notice is preferred.

Do you offer catering services?

Yes, we provide full catering services for events. Please use our enquiry form for catering requests and menu options.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and SnapScan. For wholesale accounts, we offer 30-day payment terms.
